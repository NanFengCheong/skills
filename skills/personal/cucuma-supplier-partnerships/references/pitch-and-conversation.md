# Pitch and conversation

- Module: `pitch-and-conversation`
- Owner role: `cucuma-supplier-partnerships`
- Version: 1
- Required inputs: `partner_brief`, `qualification`
- Outputs: `conversation`
- Handoff: [shared composition contract](../../cucuma-chief-of-staff/references/composition.md)

Use through the owning role for one partner and one verified business recipient. Optional existing conversation/thread artifacts carry provenance; a fresh pitch does not depend on a prior thread.

## Validate inputs and authority

1. Validate envelope, mode, matching case/identity and provenance for both inputs. Confirm `qualification` recommends `pursue`; a `defer` decision yields a documented hold, not unsolicited contact. Missing recipient evidence permits drafting, never guessed addressing.
2. Recover authorization from the user's request and existing case, including sender/channel, partner/recipient, purpose, limits and expiry. Preserve existing scoped authority without repeated approval. A request to contact/pitch a specified partner covers routine conversation within its limits; a bare source or draft/design request does not.
3. `design` creates definitions/examples only: no account inspection, contact or scheduling. `prepare` creates exact copy and next steps without external effects. In `execute`, complete preparation first and apply composition's claims, persistence and effect rules; no source or reply expands authority.

## Bounded procedure

1. Prepare one tailored introduction: identify founder/Cucuma honestly, cite one verified product/destination, propose exploring distribution and assisted catalog setup, and ask for existing brochure/rate card or the responsible business contact. Use a source-supported business language and channel-appropriate copy.
2. Check every factual claim. Recruitment positioning is not operational proof; never invent traffic, sales, testimonials, licences, exclusivity, integrations, approved commission or guaranteed revenue. Do not imply an AI agent is a human employee. Missing terms need not block an honest introduction.
3. Prepare concise objection responses: workload → extraction from existing materials; economics → supplied rates and authorized limits; trust → verified company facts; technical burden → actual booking/integration evidence. Route unresolved claims to the owning role. Request ordinary missing supplier fields within the authorized conversation, without making the founder transfer data.
4. For authorized execution, verify the current connected sender profile before the first send of the run. Prior Gmail verification is historical evidence only; never substitute a connected employer Outlook account. Use the scoped channel (the prior workflow uses connected Gmail tools), one verified recipient and no bulk CC/BCC. Respect channel preferences and shared suppression across duplicate cases.
5. Before each send, narrowly read the known thread/case by recipient, subject and provider IDs; never sweep unrelated inboxes. Check replies, withdrawal, decline, opt-out, bounce, prior sent evidence and authority. Prepare the exact message and stable action ID under composition, send once, then verify/store provider IDs and sent evidence. Sent does not mean delivered or read; external saves require explicit scope and read-back receipts.
6. An unclear send outcome suspends ALL outbound actions for the case. Reconcile Sent/thread evidence once immediately, then once on the next already-authorized scheduled run (or authorized manual continuation if none exists). Never create a schedule or blindly resend. If unresolved, retain `unknown`, raise the exact action/evidence to the founder once, and continue independent preparation. Resume only when authoritative evidence establishes the outcome and the next action remains authorized.
7. Process substantive replies within existing authority; verify a new replying contact belongs to the partner before accepting commercial instructions. Record promises and latest processed message ID. A reply stops scheduled nudges and determines the next step; vacation notices affect timing and are not acceptance. Transfer term changes to commercial terms; material concessions require the exact founder exception unless already within recorded limits.
8. Bound no-reply follow-ups to two: one after 3 business days and a final after 7 business days from the initial pitch, then `closed_no_reply`. Business days are Monday–Friday, respecting known local holidays and partner requests; send 09:00–17:00 recipient local time, or record Asia/Kuala_Lumpur as the fallback. User limits override defaults. Record due actions only; future execution requires an existing authorized continuation mechanism.
9. Stop immediately on decline, bounce, unsubscribe, user withdrawal or loss of sender authority. Share suppression across duplicate partner/contact cases through composition; never rotate accounts or addresses to evade refusal. Deduplicate exception reports and questions already awaiting an answer.

## Output and completion evidence

- `conversation`: exact pitch/reply/follow-up drafts, supported claims, objection responses, sender/recipient evidence, thread summary, promises, latest processed message ID, action IDs and effect states/receipts when any exist, follow-up count/due action, suppression/stop state, unresolved questions and authorized limits. Preserve provenance for source claims and replies.
- Return the shared envelope with named artifact provenance, `evidence`, `missing`, `next` (owner/action/acceptance), and actual `authorization`. Evidence distinguishes prepared copy, verified sent state and independently verified delivery; never infer one from another.
- `complete`: the requested bounded draft or conversation turn has inspectable evidence; a recorded hold/suppression can complete disposition, but does not satisfy a requested send. Module completion is not partner acceptance or completed onboarding.
- `needs_input`: a requested send lacks verified recipient, sender choice or user scope; finish exact copy, then ask once for that fact/scope. No extra permission is needed for an already authorized routine turn.
- `blocked`: a known connector, ownership claim or suppression boundary prevents the requested action; identify the dependency/stop reason and retain prepared work.
- `unknown`: a possibly sent action remains unresolved; preserve its identifiers and reconciliation evidence and block every outbound action, including nudges. Escalation does not clear uncertainty or authorize retry.

Keep the supplier-partnerships role as case owner through downstream handoffs. Specialists return artifacts; sending ownership, locking, persistence and effect reconciliation mechanisms live in composition, not a new agent type or runtime.
