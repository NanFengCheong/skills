# Five steps ahead

Anticipate the whole business outcome, then compose only the steps needed now. Reuse the 20 role skills through the [composition contract](composition.md) and [recipe registry](recipes.json). Detailed procedures live in their owning modules, not in this coordinator.

## Anticipation questions

1. What result does the founder want, and what can be extracted or inferred without another question?
2. What evidence does the next role need to accept this output?
3. What customer, financial or operational obligation starts if this succeeds?
4. What is the likely exception, its owner and its stopping condition?
5. What repeated work can be eliminated, and what evidence would justify expansion or stopping?

Make reversible drafting choices sensibly. Distinguish assumptions from facts. Ask only for missing material decisions; do not require founder transcription, rewriting or file transfer between roles.

## Development follows demonstrated needs

When a business step reveals a repeated manual bottleneck, pass the concrete example to product manager: trigger, existing workaround, affected users and acceptance evidence. Reuse existing capabilities. Product manager → relevant architecture/UX → disjoint backend/web/mobile work → quality/security → authorized platform delivery follows the [existing engineering workflow](workflow.md). Do not build a speculative CRM, parser service or campaign engine during skill authoring.

## Improve incrementally

Capture the failing scenario beside the affected module's ID in [composition scenarios](composition-scenarios.json). Change that module and its revision, validate its contract and affected recipes, then review the scenario. Coordinators stay unchanged unless routing changes. Shared contract changes need consumer review; do not silently replay external effects after an update.

Design-only requests end with completed artifacts. Prepare mode ends with usable drafts. Execute mode ends with evidence of the requested effects or precise unresolved blockers. No mode requires the founder to shuttle information between agents.
